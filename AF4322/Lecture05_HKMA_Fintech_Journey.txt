=== WHAT IS HKMA? ===

Core Mandate:
Hong Kong's de facto central bank, ensuring monetary & banking stability.

Strategic Enabler:
Actively shapes the market to promote fintech innovation and efficiency.

Key Pillars for Fintech:
- Future-focused Strategist:
  Publishes blueprints (Fintech 2025 & 2030) to guide ecosystem's long-term development
- Infrastructure Builder:
  Develops critical public platforms (Faster Payments System, Commercial Data Interchange/CDI)
- Fintech Supervisory Sandbox (FSS) & Suptech:
  Provides controlled environment for testing new technologies; uses tech to enhance supervision
- Ultimate Objective:
  Future-proof Hong Kong's status as a leading, safe, and innovative international financial centre

=== PART 1: A NEW ERA OF SMART BANKING ===
===========================================================

Smart banking = meeting customer needs with tailor-made and convenient services
by employing state-of-the-art technology.

- Reduce regulatory frictions hindering technological innovations
- Seven initiatives announced by HKMA in September 2017


=== THE SEVEN SMART BANKING INITIATIVES ===

1. Faster Payment System (FPS)
   - Open participation to both banks and Stored Value Facility (SVF) operators
   - Enable instant payments using only cell phone number or email address
   - Available in HK Dollar or Renminbi, 24/7
   - Industry-wide working group developed unified QR code standard

2. Enhanced Fintech Supervisory Sandbox (FSS)
   - Create Fintech Supervisory Chatroom for early-stage feedback
   - Permit tech companies to use sandbox directly
   - Interconnect HKMA, SFC, and Insurance Authority sandboxes (single application point)
   FSS Versions:
   - 1.0: Launched September 2016
   - 2.0: Upgraded 2017
   - 3.0: Launched November 2021
   - 3.1: Opened for application in 2024

3. Promotion of Virtual Banking
   - Actively promote entry of Virtual Banks (VBs) into HK financial sector
   - Updated 2000 Guide to Authorization of Virtual Banks
   - Better aligned regulations with modern digital banking needs

4. Banking Made Easy (BMES) Initiative
   Reduces regulatory friction within digital customer experience:
   - Digital customer onboarding (remote)
   - Wealth management services delivered online
   - Digital financing and lending
   - AML / CFT monitoring technologies
   - Regtech for prudential risk management and compliance
   - Research into machine-readable regulatory frameworks

5. Open Application Programming Interface (API)
   - Establishes policy framework to govern Open API
   - Promotes development and broader implementation across banking sector
   - Encourages innovation and collaboration between banks and tech firms

6. Closer Cross-border Collaboration (XB)
   - Recognizes borderless connectivity as core characteristic of fintech
   - Intensifies HKMA collaboration with regulators across jurisdictions
   - Joint initiatives with Office of Financial Development Service of Shenzhen Municipal Government
   - Distributed Ledger Technology (DLT) platform to digitalize trade finance
   - Potential future connectivity to Singapore's trade platform

7. Enhanced Research and Talent Development (ERATD)
   - Nurtures fintech talent in HK
   - Collaboration with: ASTRI, HKSTP/Science Park, Cyberport
   - Promotes new technologies for greater speed and convenience
